# Hedge planting cost sweep (4 Nov 2026)

**Why:** the calculator used one point estimate per hedge form: £10.95/m double row, £14.27/m triple row, £14.52/m productive. That is falsely precise for a cost no contractor has quoted. It was also the *low* case of the programme build-up, which assumes bulk plants at 55% of retail. Using the low case shrinks the funding gap and flatters the additionality picture.

**Change:** planting cost (excluding fencing) is now a user choice in the calculator, under "Planting costs":

- Low, Central (the default) or High; or
- Custom, where a quote can be entered in £/m.

The stock fence cost per side is also adjustable. Rounded values are used: the spread is the point, not the decimals.

| £ per metre, excl. fencing | Low | Central (default) | High |
|---|---|---|---|
| Double row, 6 plants/m (HV1, HV2, HV4, HV5, HV7) | 11 | 16 | 23 |
| Triple row, 8 plants/m (HV3, HV6) | 14 | 20 | 30 |
| Productive triple row (HV8) | 14 | 20 | 30 |
| Add for standard trees (HV4; trees toggle; HV8) | +0.25 | +2.40 | +3.20 |
| Stock fence, per side (livestock farms) | — | 9.15 (adjustable) | — |

## Basis

**Low (JUDGEMENT on CITED prices).** The programme build-up's arable, no-fence offer case (`claude/productive-hedge-farmer-cost.md`, 21 Oct 2026):

- plants at 55% of Scotplants retail;
- £0.35 spiral guard and cane per plant;
- £0.35 labour per plant;
- £1/m mulch;
- 3 years' aftercare.

**Central (JUDGEMENT).** The same build-up's central case:

- plants at 75% of retail;
- £0.50 guard and cane;
- £0.55/plant labour;
- £1.50/m mulch;
- £2/m aftercare.

That gives £20.32/m at 8 plants/m, scaled to £16.10/m at 6/m. It is cross-checked against two cited sources:

- North York Moors Traditional Boundary Fund 2026/27 standard cost: £13.46/m for planting at 6 plants/m;
- Ashridge (July 2026): BN11-compliant plant packs under £7/m, plus up to £7/m for canes, spirals and fabric, before labour.

**High (CITED).** England Capital Grants BN11 "Planting new hedges" pays £22.97/m for a double staggered row at 6 plants/m (gov.uk, 2026). This is used as a full-cost proxy. The triple row is scaled by plant count (×8/6 ≈ £30.6, rounded to £30).

**Standard trees (CITED).** England CS TE1 "Planting standard hedgerow tree" pays £19.06 per tree (gov.uk, updated July 2026). At one tree every 8 m that is £2.40/m; at one every 6 m, £3.20/m. The low case is the build-up's whip-sized standard at £0.25/m.

**Fence.** Default £9.15/m per side: the North York Moors 2026/27 standard cost (CITED). England FG1 pays £6.34/m and AECS pays £5.50/m.

**For comparison:** AECS pays £5.40/m capital for hedge planting in Scotland. That is a contribution towards cost, not a cost estimate.

## Effect (HV2, general cropping, 1 km, AECS on, 3.5%, 30 years)

| Planting cost | Funding gap without carbon (PV) |
|---|---|
| Custom £10.95/m (the previous default) | £7,184 |
| Low £11/m | £7,234 |
| Central £16/m (new default) | £12,234 |
| High £23/m | £19,234 |

At central costs, the microsite tables' funding gaps rise by about £5,000/km for double-row options and about £5,700/km for triple-row options. The tables are regenerated by `scripts/update-review-microsites.mjs`.
